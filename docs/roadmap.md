## Known limitations (Milestone 1)
- Inviting a member requires that person to already have an account;
  email invitations arrive in Milestone 2.
- No MFA yet; planned before closed beta.
- Docker is not available in every dev sandbox; docker-compose.yml is
  validated by inspection there and by CI later.
