## What shipped

### Item 1 — PlatformTeaching (T1): SUPER-admin teaching that reaches every Brain as lowest-precedence GUIDANCE

- **Model + migration** `20261103000000_sai_platform_teaching`: `PlatformTeaching` (title, content, `language` bn|en|mixed — one text + tag, the house i18n shape; `kind` style|policy|guidance; `enabled`; `sortOrder`; `createdById`/`updatedById`; timestamps). **Justification:** platform scope has no existing home — every knowledge table in the tree (KnowledgeDocument, LearnedItem, AiStoredAnswer) is org-scoped by law L1; platform guidance belongs to no org, and faking an owner org would break the isolation filters. No foreign keys (the SystemSetting precedent).
- **API** (`AdminAiController`, `/admin/ai/*`, `SuperAdminGuard` + class-level `@AdminTier("SUPER")`): `POST /admin/ai/teaching`, `PATCH /admin/ai/teaching/:id`, `PATCH /admin/ai/teaching/:id/enabled`, `DELETE /admin/ai/teaching/:id`, `GET /admin/ai/teaching`. SUPER-only harder than the admin default: even reads refuse other admin tiers, a tenant OWNER is refused by the guard's live-row check (403), and the guard's TOTP rule still applies (a SUPER without two-factor may read, never mutate). Every mutation is audit-logged (`admin.ai_teaching_create/update/enable/disable/delete`) with the actor resolved from the live admin row — never from the request body.
- **Brain wiring** (`OrgBrainService.getContext`): enabled items are read platform-wide (no organizationId — deliberate) and rendered by `renderPlatformGuidance` into a new context field **`platformGuidance`**, assembled **LAST** — after every org section in the context literal — under `PLATFORM_GUIDANCE_HEADING`, a verbatim label stating the teaching is general guidance that shapes wording/conduct only and that **the shop's own verified facts and the owner's instructions win on any conflict** (the closed D8 decision: admin global teaching ranks below org instructions on facts). Caps: 10 items, 600 chars each.
- **The channel wall (by construction, not by prompt discipline):** teaching lands ONLY in `platformGuidance` — the guidance channel beside `styleInstructions`. It is never merged into `facts`, `factsText`, knowledge matches or documents. The number guard and the Answer Store build their allowed numbers from the fact channels, so a teaching item containing a figure (the spec plants "সব দোকানে ৫০% ছাড় চলছে") **cannot ground a customer answer**: ৫০ never enters the allowed set. `buildReplyQualityBlock` transports the block verbatim after the shop's own style guidance (last, lowest precedence), wired at all four compose sites: the inbox fact-pack builder, the autopilot/order-bot compose, `suggestDraft`, and the comment compose.
- **Honest limit, stated plainly:** precedence is **structural + labelling** (last-place assembly, the yield heading, the channel wall) — it is not a mechanical conflict resolver. If a teaching line and an org fact genuinely collide in prose, the wall guarantees the teaching cannot supply *numbers or facts*; wording-level collisions are governed by the heading the model is given, exactly as the owner's own `aiInstructions` have always been.
